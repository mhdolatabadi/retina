# Clarification

- First inspect the task and relevant code or documentation. Do not ask for facts that are unambiguously available.
- Ask about missing or conflicting business rules, observable behavior, acceptance criteria, architectural decisions, and scope.
- Never silently replace missing requirements with conventions or guesses.
- Use focused questions, preferably one at a time in interactive conversations. Collect closely related questions concisely in Jira comments.
- Persist approved answers in Jira; don't rely on private chat context.
- When a material answer is unavailable, pause the affected work and report the blocker. Unrelated work may continue.
